Aggressive gynecologic cancers do not fail treatment through one mechanism. The Fuh Lab connects tumor-intrinsic targets such as AXL with stromal programs such as DDR2-dependent ferroptosis resistance—and asks which experimental system can actually resolve each question.

I built a small experiment-planning tool that turns public ovarian cell-line RNA, proteomics and culture metadata into a high/low validation pair, or an explicit warning that the question needs a different model.

How it works

Choose a Fuh-relevant marker, decide whether both RNA and protein evidence are required, and cap the doubling time you are willing to carry. The plot retains all seven ovarian-designated NCI‑60 lines. It recommends the strongest eligible contrast, then translates that contrast into a bench-ready first pass with controls.

The recommendation is deliberately allowed to fail. A missing or poorly matched model is itself an actionable result.

Analysis 2 — Keep the cell-line choice connected to patient-derived biology

Fuh-associated 2025 ovarian-cancer supplement
Where do frequently mutated input genes intersect the paper’s CompBio themes?

The deposited supplement lists 176 unique frequently mutated input genes and the genes assigned to each generated theme. Select a theme to inspect its independently computed overlap.

Select a theme

The largest displayed overlap is not treated as a new enrichment result; there is no null model here. It is a transparent way to bring a cell-line validation plan back to the extracellular-matrix, adhesion and developmental contexts observed in the paired clinical study.

Why this matters for the lab

The result is a concrete lab decision: which lines to thaw first, which baseline assay to run, and when not to use a tumor-only system. For AXL, the default matched-evidence plan favors a consistent RNA–protein contrast. For DDR2, the absence of SWATH coverage and uniformly below-panel RNA correctly redirects the work to a CAF model rather than manufacturing confidence.

What I would bring

I would bring a practical bench–analysis workflow: maintain clean, traceable cultures; use Western blotting, qPCR, flow or functional readouts with appropriate controls; and organize the resulting data so that the next experiment is easier to defend. This demo is an example of how I would connect inventory constraints, assay evidence and quantitative interpretation before committing samples and time.

Methods note

This is an exploratory planning analysis, not evidence of treatment response or causality. RNA values are relative to the full NCI‑60 panel; SWATH values remain on their distributed scale and are not numerically compared with RNA. Protein peptides are summarized by their within-gene median. Cell identity, mycoplasma status and baseline expression must be confirmed locally. Suggested perturbations and controls are prospective. Sources: NCI CellMiner, rcellminerData 2.34.0, the Fuh-associated 2025 supplement, the DDR2 study, and the Fuh Lab.
